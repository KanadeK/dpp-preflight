# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a practical, respectful place for people working across software, manufacturing, procurement, repair, and sustainability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Make technical and regulatory assumptions explicit.
- Protect confidential product and supplier information.
- Welcome contributors with different levels of domain experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's Security reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or ban contributions or participants when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
